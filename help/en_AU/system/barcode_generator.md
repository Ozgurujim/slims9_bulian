### Barcode generator

------

Enter the code that will be made into barcodes in the columns on the screen. Determine the size of the barcode (Small, Medium, or Big), and click the **Generate barcode** button. Then these will be displayed in the form of a barcode , in a popup,  and can be printed in a printer. The default encoding used is barcode 128B. You can modify this barcode encoding in the Senayan global configuration file, *sysconfig.inc.php*, and also specify it in the **System Configuration** submenu.

*Note1:*
The characters that can be processed in the Barcode generator are just the ASCII alphanumeric character set.

*Note2:*
In SLiMS 9.7.2 only Barcode encodings Code 128 and  Code 39 are functional and available to choose in System Configuration menu
