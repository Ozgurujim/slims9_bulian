### Barcode generator

------

This tool allows staff to print barcodes as they need them. Although there is an Items Barcode Printing Tool in the Cataloguing module, where barcodes would normally be printed as a batch of books are catalogued, this tool allows direct input of barcode data for printing additional barcodes as needed.

Enter the codes that will be made into barcodes in the columns on the screen. Determine the size of the barcode (Small, Medium, or Big), and click the **Generate barcode** button. Then these will be displayed in the form of a barcode , in a popup,  and can be printed in a printer. The default encoding used is barcode 128B. You can modify this barcode encoding in the Senayan global configuration file, *sysconfig.inc.php*, and also specify it in the **System Configuration** submenu.

*Note1:*
The characters that can be processed in the Barcode generator are just the ASCII alphanumeric character set.

*Note2:*
In SLiMS 9.7.2 only Barcode encodings Code 128 and  Code 39 are functional and available to choose in System Configuration menu
