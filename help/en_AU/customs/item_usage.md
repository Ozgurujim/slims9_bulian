#### Item usage

------

This is a report that lists the item, and how many times the copy has been borrowed on a monthly basis. Item usage also can be filtered by:
- *Title/ISBN,* 
- *Item code, or* 
- *Year.*

Leaving these fields blank will produce a report of all items in the collection

The report can be sorted by clicking on the headings of columns *Item code*  and *Title*

Clicking the **Print current  page** button will produce a popup preview, and your system's print controls, before you finally print.
