#### Loan list by member

------

This is a report that contains a list of items that are still borrowed by members.

You can search for members by ID or name

You can restrict the report by:

- *Membership type*
- *Loan date range*

and you can specify the number of records to show per page.

Records show member details including contact information, the items borrowed, the loan date and the due date.

Clicking the **Print current  page** button will produce a popup preview, and your system's print controls, before you finally print.
