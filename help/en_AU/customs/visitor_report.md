### Visitor statistics

------

This is a report that contains statistics of library visitors. This report contains:
- *Member Type,* and 
- *Number of visits* in each month in the year specified. 

Choose the year from the drop-down list, and then apply the filter. 

Clicking the **Print current  page** button will produce a popup preview, and your system's print controls, before you finally print.

Clicking the **Show in chart/plot** produces a popup chart, which you may screenshot if desired.
