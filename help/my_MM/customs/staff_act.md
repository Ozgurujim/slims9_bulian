

#### Staff activities

------

 This menu displays the activities of library staff who have accounts in the Senayan application. The information displayed includes:

- *User name,* 
- *Login Name,* 
- *Cataloguing data entry,* 
- *Item data entry,* 
- *Member data entry, and* 
- *Circulation.* 

This menu displays what staff members are doing and how often. To enhance the accuracy of the information, a filter is also provided that allows you to view activities from the start to the end date (as specified).

Clicking the **Print current  page** button will produce a popup preview, and your system's print controls, before you finally print.
